# BD Kids Hunter

## Objective

Find interesting lots of children’s comic books in Belgium.

Ideal listing:

- around 1 € per comic book;
- sold as a lot;
- French or Dutch;
- good condition;
- located in Belgium;
- reasonable shipping costs.
