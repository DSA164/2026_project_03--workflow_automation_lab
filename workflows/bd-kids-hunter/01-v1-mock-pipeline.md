# V1 — Mock Pipeline

## Workflow

```text
Manual Trigger
  ↓
Generate Mock Listings
  ↓
Normalize Listings
  ↓
Estimate BD Count
  ↓
Detect Language
  ↓
Detect Condition
  ↓
Compute Price Per BD
  ↓
Score Listings
  ↓
Sort Best Deals
  ↓
Filter Alerts
```

## Success criteria

- Mock listings are generated.
- Listings are normalized.
- Count is estimated when possible.
- Price per comic book is calculated.
- Language and condition are detected.
- Score is explainable.
- Best deals are sorted first.
